Name generator/Carriers/Apparel

Worn on the body

Fabric takes the largest names of any carrier here — three characters sit comfortably across a chest — and it also changes the character itself, because thread and ink do not draw a stroke the same way.

Characters1–3
Stroke limit≤ 18
Layouthorizontal

Which characters work here

Three characters at 18 strokes or fewer work in print. Embroidery is stricter: the needle moves in fixed directions, so long diagonals and thin horizontals distort, and a character built from them — 必, 之 — can emerge unrecognisable at small scale.

Why it is laid out this way

Horizontal across the chest or the panel, because a garment is read while facing the wearer. A vertical column on a tote works; a vertical column on a chest reads as a stripe rather than as writing.

Before you commit

The fabric weight decides the detail. A heavy cotton tee holds a clean print; a lightweight scarf or a knit cap loses fine strokes to the weave, so the name has to be simplified rather than shrunk.
