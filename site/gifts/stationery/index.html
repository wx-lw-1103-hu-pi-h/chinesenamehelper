Paper has no size limit

This is where the dense characters belong. A mounted scroll, a notebook cover or a bookplate gives the name the area it needs, so the choice stops being about legibility and starts being about composition.

Characters1–3
Stroke limit≤ 20
Layoutvertical

Which characters work here

All 20 strokes work here. 曦, 馥 and 澜 are at their best on paper, and this is the only carrier where a three-character name has room to breathe rather than being stacked.

Why it is laid out this way

Vertical, top to bottom, right to left — the direction a Chinese column has always run. On a notebook cover the name can run horizontally, but a scroll that is read horizontally reads as decoration rather than as writing.

Before you commit

The paper is the constraint, not the character. A scroll is mounted on silk or paper and rolled, so a name brushed on thin stock will crack at the fold; the mounting has to be planned before the writing happens.
