Name generator/Gifts/Folding fan

A column of characters on an arc

The fan is the carrier people reach for when they want height rather than width — a leaf gives a column of characters room to stand up, in a form that has carried inscriptions for centuries.

Characters1–4
Stroke limit≤ 18
Layoutvertical

Which characters work here

Four characters at 18 strokes or fewer sit along a leaf without crowding, which makes the fan the most generous carrier for a full name short of paper. A single character looks lost on a leaf; this is a carrier for two to four.

Why it is laid out this way

The inscription runs along the arc, following the leaf rather than the page. Laying it straight on a mounted fan is the mistake that makes a fan look printed rather than painted.

Before you commit

A folding fan is only inscribed on one side, and the ribs pass behind the paper — the writing has to be planned around the folds, not applied and then discovered to cross them.

Characters that suit this carrier

These characters are 18 strokes or fewer, so they hold at this size.
