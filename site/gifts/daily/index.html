Name generator/Carriers/Mug and bottle

The objects you touch daily

A mug, a bottle, a phone case: the carriers with the most use and the least ceremony. Print and engraving both work here, and both fail in different ways.

Which characters work here

Up to 14 strokes stay sharp under print. Engraving on a bottle or a case cuts a fixed depth regardless of stroke width, so very fine strokes can fill in or flake at the edges over years of handling.

Why it is laid out this way

Horizontal, reading left to right, because these objects are handled in a Western orientation even when the name is Chinese. A vertical column wraps around the curve on a bottle and disappears.

Before you commit

Dishwasher and pocket abrasion are the real test. A name printed on a mug that goes through a dishwasher weekly will fade faster than the same name engraved, and thin horizontal strokes are the first to go.
